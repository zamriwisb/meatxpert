Handling 18 September 2026

How to thaw it properly

Move the meat to the chiller 24 hours before you cook. It thaws slowly and comes back to a texture close to the day it was cut. Never use hot water or a microwave — both cook the edges and wreck the cells.

Read more

Kitchen 11 September 2026

The tools that actually matter

A heavy pan that holds its heat, tongs instead of a fork, and a probe thermometer. That is the whole list. And resting the steak is not optional — five minutes, minimum.

Read more

Butchery 4 September 2026

What silver skin is

The silver membrane on a tenderloin never breaks down, however long you cook it. A good butcher trims it off before it reaches you. If it is still on, slide a thin knife under it at an angle.
