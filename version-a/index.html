Four rooms we buy from

We name the farm because you should be able to check it.

Kagoshima

Kuroge Washu raised 30 months on the southern tip of Kyushu. Grand champion prefecture at the 2022 Wagyu Olympics. Our A5 ribeye and striploin come from a single co-operative near Satsumasendai.

Japan · A5, BMS 11–12

Miyazaki

Four consecutive national championships. Cleaner, less waxy fat than Kagoshima, with a lower melting point that suits thin yakiniku cuts more than a thick-cut steak.

Japan · A5, BMS 10–12

Darling Downs

Fullblood and F1 wagyu on 400-day grain rations in Queensland. Slaughtered under halal certification recognised by JAKIM, which is why this is the range most Malaysian households actually cook mid-week.

Australia · MB5–MB9, halal

Our own cabinet

A dry-ageing room at 1°C and 82% humidity, running a 45-day cycle on grass-fed Angus. We trim the crust ourselves and sell the pellicle to two restaurants in Bangsar for stock.

How it reaches you still frozen

Nothing leaves the room above −18°C, and nothing sits overnight in a hub.

  1. Cut at 6am

    Your primal is broken down and vacuum sealed the morning of dispatch, never the night before.

  2. Packed in dry ice

    Insulated liner plus dry ice rated for 36 hours, with a temperature strip you can read on arrival.

  3. Delivered same day

    Klang Valley by our own refrigerated van. Penang, Johor and East Malaysia by next-day air freight.

Free delivery on baskets over RM 350. Orders placed after 2pm ship the following morning.
