Halal handling, kept separate end to end

Our Australian and New Zealand range is slaughtered under certification recognised by JAKIM. It is stored in its own chiller, cut on its own bench with its own knives, and packed by a separate team. The Japanese range is not halal and never shares a surface with it. Every halal order ships with its certificate and batch number.
