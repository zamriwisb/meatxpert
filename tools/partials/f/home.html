Know your cut 28 September 2026

Picanha, and why the fat stays on

The rump cap’s thick layer of fat is the point: it bastes the meat on the grill. Score it, salt it well, cook fat side first and slice thin across the grain.

Shop picanha

Handling 18 September 2026

How to thaw it properly

Move the meat to the chiller 24 hours before you cook. It thaws slowly and comes back close to the day it was cut. Never use hot water or a microwave — both cook the edges.

Read more

Slow cook 11 September 2026

The cuts that make the best sup

Shin, tetel and soup bones are full of collagen. Simmer them low for three hours and the broth turns rich and glossy, and sets in the fridge overnight.
