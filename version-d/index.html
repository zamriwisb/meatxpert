Don't waste a good cut

The three things people ask us at the counter every week.

How to thaw it properly

Move the meat to the chiller 24 hours before you cook. It thaws slowly and comes back to a texture close to the day it was cut.

Never use hot water or a microwave. Both cook the edges and wreck the cells.

The tools that actually matter

A heavy pan that holds its heat, tongs instead of a fork, and a probe thermometer. That is the whole list.

Resting the steak is not optional. Five minutes, minimum.

What silver skin is

The silver membrane on a tenderloin never breaks down, however long you cook it. A good butcher trims it off before it reaches you.

If it is still on, slide a thin knife under it at an angle.
